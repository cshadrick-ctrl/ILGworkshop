ILG Brand Strategy Workshop

Ammunition x ILG · 20 October 2026 · 60 minutes

Purpose of the session

To align on the future of ILG, understand what makes the business genuinely different and establish the foundations for a brand strategy that can support its ambitions for years to come.

We’ll start by revisiting the strategic thinking presented during the pitch, before exploring the business’s origins, current strengths, competitive position and long-term ambitions.

The aim is to understand what ILG stands for today, what it wants to become and what it can credibly own in the market.
